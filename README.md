# Business Analytics – Research Assignment 2

## Overview

This repository contains my **Business Analytics Research Assignment 2**, completed as part of my BrightLearn Business Analytics — Foundations course.

The assignment focuses on building a practical vocabulary of important **Business Analytics concepts, data concepts, KPIs, customer and sales measurements, and marketing campaign analytics**.

The purpose of the assignment was not only to define each term, but also to understand how the concepts are applied in real business situations.

## Assignment Objectives

This assignment focuses on:

* Understanding the core vocabulary used in Business Analytics.
* Distinguishing between commonly confused analytics terms.
* Understanding how businesses measure performance using KPIs.
* Connecting analytical concepts to real-world business situations.
* Developing a stronger foundation for further Business Analytics studies.

## Topics Covered

### 01. Foundational Concepts of Analytics

This section covers the fundamental concepts behind how businesses use data to understand performance and make decisions.

* Business Analytics
* Data Analytics
* Business Intelligence (BI)
* Data-Driven Decision Making
* Big Data
* Descriptive Analytics
* Diagnostic Analytics
* Predictive Analytics
* Prescriptive Analytics
* Data Literacy

### 02. Data, Measurement & Reporting

This section focuses on how data is structured, measured, visualised and reported.

* Structured Data
* Unstructured Data
* Data Warehouse
* Data Mining
* Data Visualization
* Dashboard
* Metric
* Key Performance Indicator (KPI)
* Benchmark
* Segmentation

A particular focus is placed on understanding the difference between a **metric and a KPI**, since not every metric is a KPI.

### 03. Customer & Sales KPIs

This section explores measurements used to understand customer acquisition, customer behaviour, retention and sales performance.

* Conversion Rate
* Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
* Customer Lifetime Value (CLV / LTV)
* Churn Rate
* Customer Retention Rate
* Net Promoter Score (NPS)
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Sales Funnel

The assignment also examines the relationship between **CLV and CAC**, including the CLV:CAC ratio and why it can be useful when evaluating customer acquisition.

### 04. Marketing & Campaign Analytics

This section covers the measurement and evaluation of marketing campaigns.

* Marketing Campaign
* Post-Campaign Analysis
* Incremental Revenue
* Return on Investment (ROI)
* Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
* Click-Through Rate (CTR)
* Bounce Rate
* A/B Testing
* Lead Generation
* Marketing Attribution

These concepts demonstrate how different marketing measurements can work together to evaluate campaign performance and understand where results are coming from.

## How Each Term Was Researched

Each of the 38 terms was approached using three components:

1. **Definition** – A clear explanation of the concept in my own words.
2. **Real-World Example** – An example showing how the concept can be applied in a business or industry.
3. **Source** – A reference to the credible source used during the research.

This structure follows the requirements provided in the BrightLearn assignment brief.

## Key Learning

This assignment helped me understand that Business Analytics is not only about working with numbers. It also involves understanding **what the numbers represent, why they matter to a business, and how they can be used to support decisions**.

One of the main areas of focus was learning to distinguish between concepts that are closely related, such as:

* Business Analytics vs. Data Analytics
* Metric vs. KPI
* CAC vs. CLV
* Churn Rate vs. Customer Retention Rate
* ROI vs. ROAS
* Descriptive vs. Diagnostic vs. Predictive vs. Prescriptive Analytics
